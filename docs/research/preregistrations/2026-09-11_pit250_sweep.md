# Pre-Registration: pit250 Point-in-Time Universe Sweep (2026-09-11)

**Status: REGISTERED — no runs launched.** Registered per the convention in

## Background and hypothesis

The liquid50 pilot (`2026-09-10_liquid50_pilot.md`, results in
`docs/research/pilot_liquid50_results.md`) did not certify (best strict DSR
0.881 vs 0.95 at 73 trials) and showed that breadth alone is not the binding
constraint at 50 large caps. But it produced one genuine, directional
finding: the insider/institutional block — which subtracted ≈1.0 Sharpe at
10 mega-caps (`wfo_sweep_3d_results.md`) — **flipped positive at 50 names**
(B−A margins +0.12, −0.07, +0.16, +0.19; 3 of 4 positive, mean ≈ +0.10).
This is the down-cap strengthening the insider-trading literature predicts:
insider alpha concentrates where information asymmetry is largest, in
small/mid caps. The pilot prereg committed, in advance, that no claim would
be extended to a ~250-name universe until point-in-time (PIT) constituency
existed. That machinery now exists (see Universe below), so the real test
can be registered: mid-caps in the cross-section, honest constituency, no
survivorship shortcut.

**H1 (primary)**: the insider-inclusive signal class — LightGBM
cross-sectional rank models on the full `breadth_v1` feature set, insider
and institutional groups **included** — certifies (strict DSR > 0.95) on a
250-name point-in-time universe. On the pilot's evidence, insider-inclusive
is now the **default** configuration of the signal class, not the challenger:
both primary arms include the block, and a single insider-**excluded** arm is
retained only as the attribution control.

Both outcomes are informative. If a config certifies, it is the platform's
first certified alpha — and because constituency is PIT, the first result
eligible to be represented as investable under the pilot prereg's
requirement. If nothing certifies **and** the insider-attribution margin at
250 names does not exceed the pilot's +0.10, then the down-cap gradient
(−1.0 at 10 names → +0.10 at 50 → measured here at 250) has been given its
best pre-registered shot at real breadth under honest accounting, and the
verdict is that the next lever is IC (signal quality), not universe size.
No "tweak and retry" escape is reserved.

## Universe: point-in-time membership

`configs/universes/pit250_membership.parquet` — a ~250-name point-in-time
membership table (built in a parallel branch; this prereg binds to the
parquet as committed at launch time), accessed exclusively through
`research/universe/membership.py`:

- `symbols_as_of(date, membership=None) -> list[str]`
- `all_symbols(membership=None) -> list[str]`
- `is_member(symbol, date) -> bool`

### PIT eligibility rule (binding)

A symbol participates in cross-sectional rank normalization, model-training
rows, and portfolio construction **only on dates where
`is_member(symbol, date)` is true**. Precisely:

1. **Panel masking before ranking.** The (date, symbol) feature panel is
   masked to member rows *before* cross-sectional rank normalization, so
   each date's ranks are computed over that date's members only. Non-member
   rows do not exist anywhere downstream — not in ranks, not in training,
   not in scoring, not in weights.
2. **Entry.** A symbol becomes eligible on its membership entry date. No
   pre-entry history is backfilled; as in the pilot, absent history is
   skipped, never fabricated.
3. **Exit and delisting (unwind at last available close).** When a symbol's
   membership ends — index exit or delisting — it leaves the eligible set
   from the first non-member date: it receives no new weight from any
   decision made on or after that date. The position it holds is treated as
   unwound at its **last available close**: the final close-to-close return
   credited to the symbol is the last one computable strictly inside its
   membership spell; the bar on which no next in-spell close exists
   contributes zero return (a cash-equivalent exit at the last observed
   close). No return is ever computed across a membership gap or past a
   delisting, and no stale or fabricated prices are used.
4. **Training targets respect spells.** Forward-return targets (5d/21d,
   SPY-residualized) are computed within a symbol's contiguous membership
   spell only. A row whose h-bar-forward close falls outside its spell has a
   NaN target and is dropped from training (the standard NaN-row drop). A
   re-entry after an exit starts a new spell.
5. **SPY sidecar.** SPY remains a non-tradable residualization sidecar
   exactly as in the pilot; it is exempt from the membership mask and
   dropped before training/portfolio construction.

## Data-coverage honesty (delisted names)

The fundamentals and insider features come from the financialdatasets.ai
(FD) vendor feed. **FD coverage of fundamentals and insider transactions
for DELISTED tickers is unverified at registration time.** If dead names
lack this data, their feature columns are NaN: cross-sectional ranking
NaN-skips per column, training drops NaN rows, and prediction scores those
rows neutral (0.0) — the machinery degrades gracefully. But the scientific
cost is not graceful: if delisted-name insider coverage proves
*systematically* absent, the insider signal's PIT test is weakened, because
the very block under test would then be evaluated mostly on survivors.

**Commitment**: the results document and receipt will report the realized
coverage numbers either way — per feature group (insider, institutional,
fundamental), the share of member rows with usable (non-null) data, split
by whether the symbol is still a member at the end of the span (proxy for
listed/delisted). If delisted-name insider coverage is materially absent,
that limitation is stated in the verdict with the same prominence as the
headline number.

## Arms and exact configs

Feature set: `configs/feature_sets/breadth_v1.yml` (same stored features as
the pilot; feature memory / max lookback 252 via mom_12m_excl_1m).

Two column variants, derived from the YAML groups exactly as in the pilot:

- **insider-inclusive** (the default): all breadth_v1 groups, including the
  four insider and two institutional columns.
- **control**: breadth_v1 minus the insider and institutional groups —
  identical to the pilot's arm-A definition.

Model grid: **LightGBM only, at the incumbent regularization (λ=0.1)**. The
pilot's ridge configs were ≈ flat under breadth and are dropped; the lgbm
class supplied both the pilot's best base and best overall result
(lgbm_21d, vt10 overlay: 1.157 / DSR 0.881) and its clearest breadth
improvement over dev10 (lgbm_5d: +0.23 arm A / +0.39 arm B). Three base
configs:

1. `lgbm_21d` — lgbm, 21d horizon, λ=0.1, insider-inclusive.
2. `lgbm_5d` — lgbm, 5d horizon, λ=0.1, insider-inclusive.
3. `lgbm_21d_no_insider` — lgbm, 21d horizon, λ=0.1, control columns. The
   **attribution control**: its Sharpe subtracted from `lgbm_21d`'s is the
   insider/institutional margin at 250 PIT names, the third point on the
   down-cap gradient after −1.0 (10 names, 3d) and +0.10 (50 names, pilot).

### Overlays (2 trials, choice rule committed now)

Both overlays apply to the **best base config by pooled OOS Sharpe among
the three base trials** (a mechanical rule; if the control wins, the
overlays ride it and that is itself an attribution result):

- **(a) `_vt10` — 10% vol target** (incumbent ALPHA-3 risk layer), the
  overlay that produced both certified-adjacent results to date (0.921 at
  63 trials on dev10; 0.881 at 73 on liquid50). 5bp costs re-charged on the
  scaled weights' turnover, per fold, strictly causal — identical mechanics
  to the pilot.
- **(b) `_rcvt` — regime-conditioned vol target**: the
  `research/portfolio/risk_layer.py` regime-conditioning path with
  `RegimeConditioningConfig` **defaults** (feature `spy_iv_zscore_60d` from
  regime_features_v2; high_target 0.15 when calm, low_target 0.05 when
  stressed; z-score band [−1.0, +1.0], linear interpolation between;
  `enabled=True` — every other field at its dataclass default). The
  conditioning value is the previous bar's reading (the risk layer's t−1
  convention); bars with no admissible lagged reading fall back to the
  unconditioned 10% target. This is the regime-conditioning layer's
  **first pre-registered trial**. Motivation, committed here: the
  twice-replicated fold-4 finding (`docs/research/wfo_sweep_3d_results.md`,
  `docs/research/wfo_sweep_4b_results.md`) that slow features degrade
  totals as raw policy observations (−0.9 to −1.1 mean) while improving
  the final, recent-regime fold (+1.1 to +1.3) — both documents predicted
  a controller-layer use of slow regime features. This trial is that
  prediction, spent against the clock.

No other configs, horizons, regularization values, feature subsets, or
overlays may be run under this registration.

## Geometry and execution

- **Span**: 2020-01 .. present.
- **WFO**: anchored, 4 folds, train_window 250 — matched to the pilot and
  the full-span incumbent.
- **Label horizon**: 21 bars; **purge**: 253 bars (1 + max feature lookback
  252, mom_12m_excl_1m) — identical to the pilot.
- **Execution**: `execution_lag_days = 1`, `fill_timing = next_close`,
  `transaction_cost_bp = 5`. Honest-fill rules identical to the pilot; no
  same-bar fills anywhere.
- **Membership-aware evaluation**: the PIT eligibility rule above governs
  panel construction; portfolio weights are constructed per date over that
  date's scored members only, and non-members carry zero weight by
  construction.
- **Expected OOS sample**: ~860+ bars per config (matched to the pilot's
  864; the membership mask does not change the date axis).

## Success and failure criteria (committed in advance)

- **Primary / certification**: any of the 5 configs achieves **strict DSR >
  0.95** at **78 trials**. Strict means: SR0 = std(honest-fill pool
  **including these 5 trials**) × E[max] of 78 standard normals — the same
  computation as the pilot, one clock tick later. The honest-fill pool at
  registration is the 25 Sharpes recorded in
  `docs/research/receipts/pilot_liquid50.json` (`strict_deflation.pool_sharpes`:
  15 pre-pilot + the pilot's 10); with these 5 trials the pool grows to 30.
  For calibration, E[max]@78 = 2.442, and at the registered pool's
  dispersion (std 0.215) SR0 ≈ 0.525 — the final SR0 depends on the 5 new
  Sharpes and is computed by the runner, not chosen.
- **Secondary readouts (committed, reported regardless of outcome)**:
  1. **Insider-attribution margin**: Sharpe(`lgbm_21d`) −
     Sharpe(`lgbm_21d_no_insider`), reported against the gradient so far
     (−1.0 at 10 names; +0.10 mean at 50).
  2. **Per-cap-tercile Sharpe** (display-only, not a trial): per-date
     market-cap terciles from the `log_mkt_cap` feature (rank-normalized
     log_mkt_cap is monotone in raw cap per date, so terciles are
     unchanged), reporting the Sharpe of each tercile's P&L contribution —
     where the alpha lives, the direct check on the down-cap thesis.
  3. **Regime-conditioning margin**: `_rcvt` minus `_vt10` on the same
     base — the first controlled readout of the fold-4 hypothesis.
  4. **Realized delisted-name coverage** per the data-coverage section.
- **Failure is a reportable result**: if no config clears 0.95 and the
  insider margin does not exceed +0.10, the conclusion is that the
  insider-inclusive class does not certify at any tested breadth under
  honest accounting and the binding constraint is IC, not names; if the
  margin grows but nothing certifies, the verdict is a signal-quality
  program *within* the insider class (better features, not more symbols).
  Either verdict is written up with the same prominence a success would be.

## Amendment 1 (2026-09-11, pre-run): candidate-pool correction

Dated addendum per the pre-registration convention; no trials have been run.

**Finding during universe construction**: the vendor's asset directory
(active + inactive) is not a complete delisted-securities master. Its
inactive list covers older delistings (16 made membership: XLNX, CERN, CELG,
AGN, RTN, TIF, ...) but omits most 2022+ departures — verified absent:
TWTR, ATVI, SIVB, FRC. The data API serves bars for these names; only the
directory omits them. Building from the directory alone would re-introduce
survivorship bias in its most results-flattering form (the 2023 bank
failures would be invisible).

**Correction**: a curated supplement (`configs/universes/pit_supplement.yml`,
29 verified symbols: major 2022-2026 M&A closings, bank failures,
take-privates, pre-rename symbols) merges into the candidate pool before
screening. **Honest residual**: curated is non-exhaustive; smaller
overlooked delistings may remain absent. The bias is reduced, not
eliminated; a licensed point-in-time constituency source remains the upgrade
path, and the results doc will state this limitation.
