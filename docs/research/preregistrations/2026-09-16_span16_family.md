## Motivation (from the concluded PIT-250 arc)

Three levers are dead: RL, breadth-as-lever (2 failures), insider/13F at
scale. The surviving honest benchmark is the *insider-free* pilot arm A
class (0.958 net with vt10 @50 names; the celebrated 1.157 arm-B number is
contaminated — see Disclosures). This family changes the two things the
evidence says to change: **statistical power** (2016-2026 span, ~2,660
bars vs 1,680 — DSR scales ~√T, un-degenerates the purge-eaten fold, adds
the 2016-2019 regime to OOS) and **the label** (factor-neutralized targets
attack the diagnosed failure: added breadth added beta, not alpha).

## Features and labels

- Feature set `clean_v1` = breadth_v1 minus insider minus institutional
  groups (27 cols; identical to pit250's `lgbm_21d_no_insider` inputs).
  Regime quartet stays as model inputs. No insider/13F columns anywhere
  in this family.
- Label variant R (incumbent): forward returns residualized vs rolling
  SPY beta (as in pilot/pit250).
- Label variant N (the test): forward returns residualized vs the top-5
  PCA factors of universe daily returns, factors estimated on a trailing
  252-bar window ending at t−1, eligibility-masked (members only), loadings
  re-estimated each rebalance date. Strictly causal; no sector vendor
  dependency (FD sector facts verified missing for old delisted names,
  e.g. EMC).

## Certification

Strict deflation, unchanged: SR0 = std(honest-fill pool [35 registered
sharpes] ∪ these 5) × E[max]@88; per-trial DSR = PSR(SR0) over the
~2,660-bar track; bar 0.95. Rough arithmetic stated for honesty: SR0 will
land near 0.53-0.55; at this track length certification needs net Sharpe
≈ 1.05. That is the gap this family must close; nothing in it is
guaranteed to.

## Kill criterion (pre-committed)

If neither N-variant beats its R-twin by ≥ 0.10 OOS Sharpe, label
engineering joins the dead-levers list and the next family must source
new *data*, not new transforms.
